TrailGuide privacy & data

TrailGuide stores its working library locally in your browser. If you sign in, Trips and Hiking Guides are synchronized to a separate trailguideUsers namespace in the configured Firebase Firestore project.

Google Drive

TrailGuide requests the drive.file scope. Files created or uploaded by TrailGuide are kept in a top-level TrailGuide folder. Travel documents and GPX files are stored in normal Google Drive folders and remain accessible outside the app.

Separation from Travel & Photography Guide

This app uses its own local-storage prefix, its own Firestore namespace and its own Drive root folder. It does not intentionally read the libraries of the photography app.
